Scope of this case

This is retrospective evaluation against previously computed DFT labels. It reports no new DFT calculations, synthesis or newly discovered materials; the fitted policy did not beat adapted greedy.

The problem

Expensive evaluations make candidate selection important. A useful materials workflow must distinguish a model estimate from a reference result and show whether its selection rule actually helps.

The mechanism

MaterialMind displays real WBM crystal structures alongside stability estimates, uncertainty and acquisition scores. Frozen pretrained CHGNet embeddings and composition descriptors feed a fitted residual ensemble. The workbench ranks a held-out candidate pool; revealing a historical DFT label updates the session, while CIF and portable-record exports preserve the geometry and decision trail.

The observed result

The study uses 3,072 existing structures, with 641 chemical-system-held-out candidates. At a budget of 40 evaluations, the fitted policy finds an average 29.6 stable candidates versus 6.7 for random selection and 30.6 for adapted greedy. The actual local workflow also reveals a reference, exports, resets and restores the session with history and candidate order intact.

Foundations

WBM structures, labels and published CHGNet predictions come from the Matbench Discovery collection by Janosh Riebesell and contributors (CC BY 4.0), with the original Wang–Botti–Marques WBM study credited. CHGNet 0.3.0 is a frozen BSD-3-Clause foundation; the residual model and acquisition study are the project’s work. Code and bundled Three.js are MIT licensed with their respective notices.

The CPU demo includes frozen graph features/source predictions and needs no model download. First startup fits the small residual model. The server binds to loopback; the primary action opens installation instructions, not a hosted laboratory.

Frozen CHGNet 0.3.0 embeddings plus 20 composition descriptors feed an ExtraTrees residual ensemble and held-out calibration. No official blind Matbench Discovery score, universal acquisition improvement or novel algorithm is established. The chosen policy stays frozen; the better greedy comparator is not relabeled as the validated policy.

Revealing a label retrieves a previously computed reference, not a new DFT solve. Imported geometry remains geometry-only without a compatible prediction/composition reference. A schema-3 record restores only under matching dataset, policy, code and scientific environment; export is not automatic persistence or arbitrary-version migration.

The recorded HTTP workflow covered reveal/export/reset/restore/reload and session isolation; controlled UI checks covered stale and failed responses. These workflow checks do not rerun all original materials experiments or establish physical discoveries.

PhotonSmith

Design a periodic photonic grating, inspect its solved electric field and spectrum, and compare the result with a separate numerical formulation.

Run locallyInspect the retained design

A grating for 1550 nm

For the retained refined 1550 nm design, the stored M9 target-order efficiency is 0.9358787717897128, about 93.59% of incident power into order +1. M13 and independent real-space checks are separately recorded. The figure pairs the same design’s field with all 21 stored wavelength solves.

Interpretation

This is a two-dimensional periodic numerical model, not a fabricated-device measurement. Agreement between the disclosed resolutions and formulations does not certify convergence, omitted physics, global optimality or manufacturing readiness.

What a surrogate can miss

An optimizer can find geometry that looks promising to a learned model while failing a physical solve or a stronger numerical check. The design needs a trace from target and geometry to field, power and validation.

From screening to Maxwell solves

PhotonSmith searches a constrained three-layer periodic grating. A compact learned surrogate screens candidates, a Fourier-modal Maxwell solver and adjoint refinement evaluate them, and a separately assembled real-space solver checks the result. The Studio keeps the target, geometry, field, spectrum and exported evidence together.

Foundations

The runtime uses NumPy and SciPy. Research foundations and optional external validation tools are separately credited in the repository; an available Meep adapter is not evidence of an executed Meep validation.

What the evidence supports

Negative snapshot; research ongoing

Scope & open questions

The report concerns fixed-feature ridge pipelines and controlled defects. Exact update identities are established methods. Later research continued without a successful new mechanism; this is the completed October report, not a claim that the broader goal succeeded.

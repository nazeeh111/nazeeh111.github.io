What the evidence supports

Theory with open utility

Positive moment completions yield exact frequency-compatibility tests and sharp growth bounds in specified axial and nonaxial models. The published counter-flux test rejects none of the reproduced closure modes, leaving practical usefulness unresolved.

Scope & open questions

These are information bounds for restricted local, collisionless fast-flavor models, rather than predictions for a supernova. Some later extensions await independent review; scientific priority remains unconfirmed.

Neutrino Moments

Neutrino frequency bounds

Which growing frequencies are compatible with limited information about a neutrino angular distribution?

Nazeeh Abdul-HadiSharp bounds; usefulness unresolvedSep 8, 2026
Paper PDF ↓Sources & citation
In this studyMethodResultsInterpretationLimitationsPaper & sources

Method

Treat each species as a positive angular measure constrained by supplied moments. In the restricted axial model, the dispersion equations reduce to a complex Stieltjes value, and classical moment theory turns compatibility into a disk-intersection problem.

Exact certificates check individual frequency decisions. Further analysis derives sharp growth envelopes, including a finite-wave-number result for equal supplied second moments and a separate nonaxial result with common isotropic full moments.

Results

CheckResult
Planned frequency decisions504 independently checked: 72 possible and 432 impossible
Continuous solver comparison722 cases; no certified disagreement, and no general end-to-end speed advantage
Published counter-flux testAll eight reproduced growing closure modes remain compatible
Practical usefulnessNot established by the closure-mode tests

Interpretation

The method bounds what the moment information permits. A compatible unstable frequency need not occur in the actual physical background, and different frequencies may need different angular completions.

Limitations

  • The models are local and collisionless. The axial and nonaxial results have different moment assumptions.
  • The later nonaxial extension has exact algebraic checks; the earlier independent verification covers the axial results.
  • Scientific priority and practical astrophysical benefit remain unestablished.

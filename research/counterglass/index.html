Counterglass

Testing stateful tool agents

Can a successful agent response conceal an incorrect database or file state?

Nazeeh Abdul-HadiSelection target missedOct 16, 2026
Paper PDF ↓Sources & citation
In this studyMethodResultsInterpretationLimitationsPaper & sources

Method

Checkpoint real SQLite and file state together with a resumable policy state. Change one declared environmental condition, continue execution, and check the resulting state independently.

The selector study compares ten policies over thirty-two seeds and three authored workflow structures. Separate ToolSandbox and local-model cases exercise the testing mechanism beyond the scripted selector evaluation.

Interpretation

The stateful failure-evidence mechanism is useful within its supported workflows. The added pooled selector does not earn a general discovery claim and loses to the simpler schema heuristic.

Limitations

  • The study holds out parameters within three authored structures, not entire unseen applications.
  • Evidence records are unsigned. Consistency checking and fresh replay do not authenticate arbitrary executions or prove every internal policy transition.

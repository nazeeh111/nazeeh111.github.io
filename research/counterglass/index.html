Counterglass: Stateful Counterfactual Failure Evidence and a Budgeted Search Study

Checkpointed tool-agent executions expose failures that successful responses can conceal.

Nov 1, 2026 · 12 pages · PDF · 195 KB

Read PDF ↗Download PDF ↓Manuscript sources ↓
What the evidence supports

Negative primary result

A locked 1,280-episode study tests ten search policies. The proposed pooled selector misses its frozen improvement target and loses to a simpler schema heuristic. All 7,177 unique recorded pairs were freshly replayed and independently checked with zero mismatches.

Scope & open questions

The evaluation uses three authored workflow structures. Separate ToolSandbox and local-model cases support execution evidence, not broad selector generalization. Evidence records are unsigned and do not prove arbitrary agent behavior.

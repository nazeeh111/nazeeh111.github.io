Manifold

Stopping rules for heat diffusion

Can spectral moments reduce the work required to approximate a sparse diffusion calculation accurately?

Practical targets missedProvisional date: Jun 17, 2025
Paper PDF ↓Sources & citation
In this studyMethodResultsInterpretationLimitationsPaper & sources

Method

Approximate the action of a matrix exponential using Chebyshev terms. Moment information supplies bounds on omitted terms; a partitioning rule chooses a tail bound.

Compare the complete calculation with ordinary Chebyshev, SciPy Taylor, restarted Hermitian Krylov, and CRAM16. A separately frozen continuation adds a fixed-envelope evaluator and a faster exact dyadic checker.

Computed quantity

y=e−tAb

A is the diffusion operator, b is the initial vector, and t is elapsed diffusion time. The algorithm computes the resulting vector without forming the full matrix exponential.

Interpretation

The continuation improves the implementation. It does not establish a generally better numerical algorithm: comparisons against the numerical baselines remain mixed, and the original scientific targets fail.

Limitations

  • The core polynomial identities have established precedents; application novelty is unresolved.
  • A floating-point counterexample narrows the supported range. The mathematical bound is not a complete floating-point certificate.

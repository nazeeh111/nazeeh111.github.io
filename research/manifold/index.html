Moment-Weighted Fourier Envelopes for Heat Actions: A Reproducible Algorithm-Discovery Study

A spectral-moment stopping rule is tested against established methods for sparse diffusion calculations.

Nov 2, 2026 · 13 pages · PDF · 154 KB

What the evidence supports

Negative primary result

The original rule saves 0–8.71% of matrix-vector products and misses both frozen practical targets. A separate engineering continuation runs 4.46–8.01 times faster than unchanged v1, primarily through checker improvements.

Scope & open questions

The numerical-baseline comparisons remain mixed. The core identities have established precedents, application novelty is unresolved, and no complete floating-point certificate or algorithmic breakthrough is claimed.

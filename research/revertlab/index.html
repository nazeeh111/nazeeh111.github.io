RevertLab: Cost-aware Continuation under Rival Spread Dynamics

A chronological pairs-trading study values convergence, relationship failure, both trading legs, and costs.

Nov 1, 2026 · 7 pages · PDF · 166 KB

What the evidence supports

Negative primary result

The proposed rule loses 1.20% on deposited capital during the reserved 2023–2025 evaluation. Its uncertainty interval against the development-selected gate includes harm, and simpler methods perform better.

Scope & open questions

The study uses six crypto assets and three candidate pairs with simulated borrowing and execution. It establishes no economic advantage or historical short availability. The retained two-leg account supports independent reconciliation.

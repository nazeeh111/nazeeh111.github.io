RevertLab

Pairs trading under relationship failure

Does valuing convergence and relationship failure together improve trading decisions after both-leg costs?

No trading advantage establishedMay 21, 2026
Paper PDF ↓Sources & citation
In this studyMethodResultsInterpretationLimitationsPaper & sources

Method

A forward-only model compares stable mean reversion with a persistent rival. It values a fixed convergence policy using actual holdings, delayed liquidation, borrowing, and execution costs.

The six-asset, three-pair crypto study uses formation-only selection, development through 2022, and a reserved 2023–2025 evaluation. A separate accountant reconstructs both legs and every cashflow.

Results

Reserved evaluationResult
Proposed-rule return on deposited capital−1.20%
Maximum drawdown4.70%
Average gross exposure2.72%
Improvement over the selected gateUncertainty interval includes harm; economic advantage unestablished

Interpretation

The integrated rule does not establish an advantage. Simpler comparators perform better, and known-process tests show that higher volatility can be mistaken for relationship failure.

Limitations

  • Borrow availability and execution are simulated assumptions. The study does not establish historical short availability or achievable venue fills.
  • The result is specific to this chronological asset universe and protocol. Positive returns in a comparator do not establish an investable edge.

inertiaforge: Auditing Contact Dynamics and the Limits of Gradient Agreement

A rigid-body engine tests whether agreement across simulation refinements makes a contact derivative trustworthy.

Nov 2, 2026 · 11 pages · PDF · 178 KB

Read PDF ↗Download PDF ↓Manuscript sources ↓
What the evidence supports

Negative primary result

The proposed gate accepts 18 of 19 smooth held-out derivatives, but only three accepted derivatives meet the accuracy target. A restricted, established event-time baseline passes all 19 supported smooth cases.

Scope & open questions

The study rejects the proposed reliability hypothesis. Its separate 5.474 mm measured-trajectory transfer result covers three bouncing-ball trials; it does not validate general contact physics or certify gradients.

FUSIONZERO

Current constraints and tokamak geometry

Does enforcing a feasible, current-conserving plasma source improve predicted equilibrium geometry?

Preliminary; utility target missedProvisional date: Mar 10, 2026
Paper PDF ↓Sources & citation
In this studyMethodResultsInterpretationLimitationsPaper & sources

Method

A fixed current-space dictionary maps source coefficients to poloidal flux. A convex correction enforces nonnegative current cells and the observed current sum while minimizing flux displacement.

The source-only development study compares those corrected predictions with direct flux regression on a discharge-separated DIII-D cohort. Earlier notes separately test contour-preserving flux relabeling and synthetic passive-current ambiguity.

Results

Development comparisonResult
Feasibility and optimality checksAll 256 constrained predictions pass

Synthetic ambiguity supplement3.5437 cm axis gap under identical declared instantaneous inputs

Interpretation

A feasible current representation does not by itself improve geometry. The tested correction loses to direct regression; the ambiguity supplement identifies a separate information limit in its synthetic setup.

Limitations

  • These are preliminary development studies from an ongoing project. There is no blind-test or cross-machine improvement.
  • The ambiguity construction is conditional and numerical, not a continuum-certified impossibility theorem or a measured experimental result.
  • No original inverse-reconstruction algorithm is established by these notes.

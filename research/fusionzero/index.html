FUSIONZERO / Preliminary notes

Current Conservation Does Not Ensure Accurate Equilibrium Geometry

Tokamak reconstruction studies separate numerical current constraints from useful predictions of equilibrium geometry.

Nov 1, 2026 · 7 pages · PDF · 199 KB

Read PDF ↗Download PDF ↓Manuscript sources ↓
What the evidence supports

Preliminary negative result

All 256 current-constrained predictions pass the declared feasibility and optimality checks, but the local reconstruction score is 0.783302 versus 0.869123 for direct flux regression. Two earlier notes retain flux-relabeling failures and a controlled passive-current ambiguity study.

Scope & open questions

This is a development snapshot of ongoing research. No original inverse algorithm, blind-test improvement, experimental ambiguity, or cross-machine performance is established.

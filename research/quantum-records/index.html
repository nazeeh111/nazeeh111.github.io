Opposite Encoding Preferences for Quantum-Record Consensus and Pooled Inference

The best encoding for separated observers can be the worse encoding when their records are read together.

Oct 29, 2026 · 10 pages · PDF · 123 KB

What the evidence supports

Restricted theoretical result

The study solves a prescribed pure-product encoder problem under a common random-rotation channel. At an exact benchmark, the optimized consensus gap is 1/16; a coherent-state realization also has rational bounds for unrestricted local measurements.

Scope & open questions

The result holds within explicit encoder and noise assumptions. Generic ordering reversal already occurs classically. Historical priority, an entanglement advantage, and experimental validation are not established.

Quantum Darwinism follow-up

The selected rebuild of the One-Shot Quantum Darwinism project. It supersedes the earlier allocation and analytic-transition drafts.

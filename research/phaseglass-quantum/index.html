Phaseglass Quantum

Two-copy quantum measurements

Can a joint measurement on two copies distinguish states that look identical to whole-block purity tests?

Nazeeh Abdul-HadiExact two-copy separationOct 23, 2025
Paper PDF ↓Sources & citation
In this studyMethodResultsInterpretationLimitationsPaper & sources

Method

Compare a periodic cluster state with an entangled dimer state under specified unknown rotations within two-qubit blocks. Keep all four symmetry-sector outcomes from a Bell measurement, rather than compressing them into a purity statistic.

An exact rational certificate bounds the competing separate-copy strategies at the same two-copy budget. A separate finite-shot simulation compares longer-budget classifiers on 400 held-out physical states.

Interpretation

The exact separation is a positive result for this specified discrimination problem. The longer-budget simulation supports the tested measurement, while the optimal many-copy single-copy strategy remains unresolved.

Limitations

  • The theorem concerns two total copies and a restricted family of symmetry-preserving rotations. It does not cover arbitrary states in either phase.
  • The model-aware eight-copy comparison was added after inspecting the test cohort, and its numerical integration is not converged at larger budgets.
  • All reported experiments use local simulation. Hardware and asymptotic advantages are unproved.

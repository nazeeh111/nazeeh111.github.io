Phaseglass Quantum / Research manuscript

Two-Copy Symmetry-Sector Discrimination Beyond Matched Block Purities

A two-copy quantum measurement distinguishes states whose whole-block purity statistics are identical.

Nov 1, 2026 · 17 pages · PDF · 250 KB

Read PDF ↗Download PDF ↓Manuscript sources ↓
What the evidence supports

Restricted positive result

On the specified eight-qubit problem, exact success is 75%, above a certified 66.91% upper bound for separate-copy measurements. At eight copies, simulated accuracy is 97.075% versus 81.600% for a model-aware reference.

Scope & open questions

The exact theorem uses two total copies and specified symmetry-preserving state families. Longer-budget comparisons are empirical. No hardware, asymptotic, or general phase-recognition advantage is established.

TAILSENTRY

Rare-loss sampling and missed failure modes

Can a structured rare-event sampler improve efficiency while keeping uncertainty meaningful when a catastrophic mode is missed?

Efficiency target missedDec 4, 2025
Paper PDF ↓Sources & citation
In this studyMethodResultsInterpretationLimitationsPaper & sources

Method

For supported maximum-of-quadratic losses, a proved union of halfspaces covers the failure regions. Exact conditional sampling and integration of one Gaussian direction produce bounded probability contributions.

The frozen evaluation contains 17,280 complete algorithm runs across eight known-truth problems and fifteen methods or variants. A separate randomized-order timing study checks the primary efficiency conclusion.

Interpretation

Conditioning improves the corresponding raw union sampler, but the strongest conditional quasi-Monte Carlo comparator wins the primary task. Large observed effective sample sizes do not protect an ordinary interval from a completely missed mode.

Limitations

  • The derivation uses established union sampling, conditional expectation, and concentration bounds. An original general estimator is not established.
  • The guarantees depend on the supported model and failure cover. The historical FX sensitivity check is separate from estimator efficiency.

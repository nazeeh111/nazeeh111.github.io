TAILSENTRY: Structure-Certified Conditional Union Sampling for Rare Losses

An auditable rare-event sampler tests probability bounds, missed catastrophic modes, and efficiency.

Nov 1, 2026 · 13 pages · PDF · 179 KB

Read PDF ↗Download PDF ↓Manuscript sources ↓Code & evidence ↗
What the evidence supports

Negative primary result

Across 17,280 complete algorithm runs, the selected candidate loses to conditional scrambled Sobol on the primary efficiency comparison. A missing-mode test also shows ordinary intervals can be badly overconfident despite large observed effective sample sizes.

Scope & open questions

The numerical results concern supported Gaussian and Student-t loss models. Conditioning improves the corresponding raw union sampler, but neither an original general estimator nor superiority to the strongest comparator is established.

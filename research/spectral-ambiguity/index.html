Spectral Ambiguity / Paper & follow-up

Projected Witness Reuse for Positive Spectral Experiment Design

Cached adversarial spectra accelerate a measurement-selection calculation, while acquisition tests fail their scientific target.

Oct 29, 2026 · 7 pages · PDF · 149 KB

What the evidence supports

Negative acquisition result

The original finite-menu study reports a 5.16× median selection speedup over warm-started HiGHS. Physical acquisition reaches the target in 4/9 cases versus 6/9 for conventional design. A corrected follow-up finds all five policies miss the target in all nine new cases.

Scope & open questions

The speedup is for a specialized computation, rather than demonstrated scientific acquisition superiority. The follow-up identifies a resolution bottleneck and leaves independent physical sampling costs unestablished. The original and corrective reports remain separate.

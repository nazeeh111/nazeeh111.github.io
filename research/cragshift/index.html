Camera–laser change detection

Does learning sensor quality improve movement detection over simple camera–laser fusion?

Nazeeh Abdul-HadiPositive simulation resultJul 13, 2026
Paper PDF ↓Sources & citation
In this studyMethodResultsInterpretationLimitationsPaper & sources

Method

Original rendered surveys and independently raycast laser observations measure a common surface-normal motion component. Two learned residual-scale models weight the sensors while keeping dependence and shared-reference uncertainty explicit.

A temporal confirmation rule misses early movement and is removed. The simpler remaining method is evaluated on sixteen fresh procedural sites with unchanged weights and calibration thresholds.

Interpretation

The fresh-site recall gain is 6.39 percentage points, with a paired site-bootstrap interval of 4.28–8.86 points. This supports learned quality weighting on the simulated task, while false-alarm equality is not established.

Limitations

  • This is the preserved simulation study, not field validation or a rockfall forecast.
  • A later realism and false-positive revision was unfinished at the saved snapshot. Its proposed changes are not treated as verified results.

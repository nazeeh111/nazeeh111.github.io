cragshift: Quality-aware Camera–Laser Monitoring with an Auditable Simulated Rock-slope Benchmark

Learned sensor-quality weighting is evaluated on original camera–laser surveys of procedural rock slopes.

Nov 2, 2026 · 6 pages · PDF · 434 KB

Read PDF ↗Download PDF ↓Manuscript sources ↓
What the evidence supports

Simulated positive result

On sixteen fresh simulated sites, recall is 82.33% versus 75.93% for simple fusion, at unchanged observation coverage. The paired site-bootstrap recall gain is 6.39 percentage points, with interval [4.28, 8.86].

Scope & open questions

False-positive rates are 5.60% and 5.42%; false-alarm equality is not established. This is a preserved simulation study. A later realism and false-positive revision was still unfinished at this snapshot; no field-safety or collapse-prediction claim is supported.
